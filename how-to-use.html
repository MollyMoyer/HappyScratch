How To Use

If your cat hasn’t started using Happy Scratch yet, that’s completely normal. With a little patience and the right approach, most cats catch on quickly. Here are some ways to help your cat use our product.

Training Tips

Placement matters. Cats like to scratch in areas where they already spend time. If your cat has been scratching at the couch, place the scratching post right next to it to show them it is there. You can use a pet-safe deterrent spray or temporarily cover problem areas with materials cats don’t like, such as double-sided tape or smooth fabric. Once they start using the post regularly, you can move it to a location that works for you.

The safely built-in catnip can draw attention to many cats. Some cats also respond well if you gently scratch the surface yourself to show them what it’s for.

Any time your cat paws at or scratches the post, reward that behavior. You can use treats or play session with them as encouragement. The more you reinforce those small moments, the likely they are to come back to the post on their own.

Consistency Is Key

Keep the post in the same spot during the first week or longer so your cat can get familiar with it. If they haven’t shown interest yet, try leading them over to it once or twice a day and gently encouraging interaction. Stick with it. Most cats begin using a new post within a few days once they realize it’s fun and rewarding.

Some cats adjust right away. Others take a little longer. That’s completely okay. What matters most is staying consistent with your kitties!
